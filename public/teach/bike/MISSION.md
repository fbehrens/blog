# Mission: Planning and navigating self-guided bike tours

## Why
I want to ride multi-day bike tours that I planned myself, following my own tracks
instead of hoping for signposts — starting with Berlin → Usedom (~340 km, Baltic
coast) as the first real test. The tour is the deadline; the skill is the point.

## Success looks like
- I can plan a multi-day tour as day-stage tracks, with sane daily distances and surfaces I actually want to ride.
- I can export those tracks and get them onto the Garmin Edge Explore 2 **and** an Android app, offline-capable.
- I can navigate a stage turn-by-turn, and recover calmly when I go off course or the route is closed.
- I can adjust a stage in the field (shortcut, detour, find water/food/accommodation) without losing the plan.
- After Berlin → Usedom, I can repeat the whole workflow for any new tour without help.

## Constraints
- First target tour: Berlin → Usedom. Hardware fixed: Garmin Edge Explore 2 + an Android phone.
- Answers short and concrete; German-language sources are fine and often better here.
- (To confirm) riding style, daily distance target, bike/tyres, accommodation style, and tour dates.

## Out of scope
- Training/fitness programming, bike mechanics, and gear reviews.
- Racing/performance metrics (power, FTP) — this is a navigation and planning mission.
