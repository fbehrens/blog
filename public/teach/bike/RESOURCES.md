# Bike Tour Planning & Navigation Resources

## Knowledge

- [Garmin Edge Explore 2 Owner's Manual (web)](https://www8.garmin.com/manuals/webhelp/GUID-E67DE53E-5B08-4903-9767-F58973BC1829/EN-US/Edge_Explore_2_OM_EN-US.pdf)
  The primary source for what the device can and cannot do. Use for: course loading, rerouting settings, navigation screens, map/POI search.
- [Garmin: Rerouting a Course (Edge Explore 2)](https://www8.garmin.com/manuals/webhelp/GUID-E67DE53E-5B08-4903-9767-F58973BC1829/EN-US/GUID-62C3249E-F56E-467B-A9DA-83D907CDF4E6.html)
  Exact behaviour when you leave the course. Use for: off-course recovery, "no U-turns" quirk.
- [Garmin Support: Importing a Third-Party Course into Garmin Connect](https://support.garmin.com/en-US/?faq=wKuZXCaZRP4mWPX5aRz5h5)
  Which file formats Connect accepts (GPX/TCX). Use for: getting a planner's export onto the device.
- [komoot: Importing GPX or FIT files manually to Garmin](https://support.komoot.com/hc/articles/360041874691)
  The USB/FIT sideload path that bypasses Garmin Connect. Use for: preserving turn instructions and course points.
- [DC Rainmaker: Garmin Edge Explore 2 In-Depth Review](https://www.dcrainmaker.com/2022/07/garmin-explore-review.html)
  Deepest independent test of this exact device. Use for: realistic expectations on navigation, battery, maps.
- [Wikivoyage: Radfernweg Berlin–Usedom](https://de.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Radfernweg_Berlin%E2%80%93Usedom)
  Neutral, non-commercial stage-by-stage description of the target route (~337 km, Museumsinsel → Peenemünde). Use for: stage planning, surfaces, alternates.
- [Berlin-Usedom-Radfernweg — Vorpommern (official regional tourism)](https://www.vorpommern.de/berlin-usedom-radfernweg.html)
  Official route info and signage. Use for: cross-checking the "real" waymarked line against your own track.
- [cycle.travel route planner](https://cycle.travel/map)
  Free planner with unusually good surface/quiet-road routing and stage splitting. Use for: sanity-checking a komoot line.
- [cycle.travel: GPS & file formats](https://cycle.travel/advice/map/gps)
  What each export contains (GPX track / GPX route / TCX course / TCX activity / KML) and the one-click **Send to Garmin Connect** path. Use for: getting stages onto the Edge.
- [cycle.travel app: Logging in](https://cycle.travel/app/help/accounts) and [app FAQ](https://cycle.travel/app/help/faq)
  Confirms one account covers web + app ("routes planned on the web are accessible in the app, and vice versa"), and that offline use needs an explicit route download plus a downloaded offline map. Use for: the phone side of redundancy.
- [BRouter-web](https://brouter.de/brouter-web/)
  Open-source OSM router with editable routing profiles. Use for: full control over surface/waytype preferences and clean GPX export.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [r/bikepacking](https://reddit.com/r/bikepacking) and [r/cycletouring](https://reddit.com/r/cycletouring)
  English-language, high-signal for route and navigation-workflow questions.
- [Radreise & Fernradler Forum](https://www.radreise-forum.de/)
  German long-distance touring forum, decades of route knowledge on exactly these Brandenburg/MV routes. Highest-value source for local conditions.
- [Garmin Forums: Edge Explore 2](https://forums.garmin.com/sports-fitness/cycling/f/edge-explore-2)
  Device-specific quirks straight from other owners. Use for: firmware behaviour, rerouting complaints.

## NRW-Bahntrassen (Lektion 4)

- [bahntrassenradeln.de — NRW](https://bahntrassenradeln.de/bahn_nw.htm)
  Verzeichnis aller Bahntrassenwege mit Länge, Belag, Tunneln und GPX pro Trasse (`/gpx/nw4_06.gpx`). Use for: jede Trasse finden und laden.
- [einfach-bergisch-radeln.de](https://einfach-bergisch-radeln.de/) — offizielle Panorama-Radwege, Touren, Downloadbereich.
- [nordbahntrasse.de](https://nordbahntrasse.de/) · [balkantrasse.de](https://www.balkantrasse.de/) — Fördervereine; Sperrungen, Karten, GPS.
- [Radroutenplaner NRW](https://www.radroutenplaner.nrw.de/) — Landesplaner mit Kategorie Bahntrassenwege.
- [radrevier.ruhr](https://www.radrevier.ruhr/) — Ruhrgebiet, Knotenpunkte, Tourenplaner.
- [Wikivoyage: Bergischer Panorama-Radweg](https://de.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Bergischer_Panorama-Radweg) — 132 km Hattingen–Olpe in 3 Etappen.
