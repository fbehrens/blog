# Mission: Sketchbook on the Galaxy S26 Ultra

## Why
Enjoy drawing with the S Pen (recommended by a friend), learn basic drawing skills, and quickly move drawings to the Mac, e.g. to use as a Claude Code prompt.

## Success looks like
- Draw confidently in Sketchbook with the S Pen (brushes, colors, undo, layers)
- Send a drawing from the phone to the Mac in under 30 seconds and use it in Claude Code
- Add comic speech bubbles and text to photos
- Write by hand on the phone and use it (notes, captions, prompts)

## Constraints
- Draws only on the S26 Ultra; Mac is only the receiver
- Beginner: little drawing experience
- Lessons small, max ~30 minutes; no deadline

## Out of scope
- Sketchbook on desktop/Mac, animation, professional illustration workflows
