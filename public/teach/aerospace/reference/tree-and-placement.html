AeroSpace layouts · reference

Tree & placement

The model

  • Window: a leaf.
  • Container: a non-leaf node, with a layout and an orientation.
  • Root container: exactly one per workspace.
  • Layouts: tiles | accordion. Orientations: horizontal | vertical.
  • The "layout of a window" is the layout of its parent container.

Source: guide#tree. Note: an i3 "container" is an AeroSpace "node".

Notation (this course)

h[1 v[2 3]]      h_tiles root: 1 | stack(2/3)
H[…]  V[…]       accordion (upper case)

Placement rule

New window Goes into the parent of the most recently focused window, at index + 1.
  • Focus decides the container and the slot.
  • A new window never creates a container.
  • If the most recent window is floating, the new window goes to the end of the root.
  • Dialogs are floating; popups aren't tiled.

MacWindow.swift → unbindAndGetBindingDataForNewTilingWindow

Normalization (on by default)

  1. Flatten: a container with one child is replaced by that child. A root with a single window is allowed.
  2. Opposite orientation: a nested container always has the opposite orientation to its parent.

guide#normalization. Both are enabled in your config.

join-with

Wrap Me + nearest node in that direction → new container, opposite orientation of their parent, at the neighbour's slot.
  • right/down: I'm first. left/up: I'm last.
  • Neighbour may be a whole container → things can rotate.

move

  1. Swap: sibling that way is a window.
  2. Move in: sibling is a container → land after its most recently focused window.
  3. Move out: climb to first ancestor with the move's orientation, land beside the container I left.

Wall: reaching the root wraps the whole tree in a new root (move's orientation). h[1 2 3] move down → v[h[1 2] 3]
