# Mission: Salsa Dancing (Linear On1, Lead)

## Why
Dance confidently at salsa socials with strangers. Previously trained to improver level but rusty — the goal is to restore and then exceed the old level so that asking anyone to dance feels easy, not risky.

## Success looks like
- Find the "1" in any salsa song within one phrase, without watching other dancers
- Lead a full social dance (basic, turns, cross-body lead variations) cleanly on time, with a follower I've never danced with
- Maintain a repertoire of ~10 reliable combinations recalled under social-dancing pressure
- Attend local socials regularly and enjoy them

## Constraints
- Practices solo at home, with a partner, and at local classes/socials
- Style: Linear On1 (LA style), dancing as lead
- Short, focused lessons preferred

## Out of scope
- On2 / Cuban casino / rueda (for now)
- Performance/choreography work
- Following (lead only, for now)
