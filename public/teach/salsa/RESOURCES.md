# Salsa (On1 Lead) Resources

## Knowledge

- [Article: "How to Find the 'One'… in Salsa Music" — salsacantante](https://salsacantante.com/blog/how-to-find-the-one/)
  Instrument-by-instrument guide (conga, cowbell, piano montuno) to locating beat 1. Use for: musicality and timing.
- [Article: "How To Find The 1 Beat In Salsa Music" — GO Latin Dance](https://golatindance.com/how-to-find-that-damn-1-beat-in-salsa/)
  Practical ear-training drills for the 1. Use for: timing practice routines.
- [Article: "Clave and Percussion in Salsa Music" — DanceInTime](https://danceintime.com/miscellaneous-1/2017/3/22/clave-and-percussion-in-salsa-music)
  2-3 vs 3-2 clave, percussion roles. Use for: deeper music structure.
- [App: BeatLab Salsa Rhythms](https://beatlab.app/en/salsa/)
  Interactive rhythm trainer with isolated salsa instruments. Use for: solo ear training between lessons.
- [App: Salsa Beat Machine](https://salsabeatmachine.org/) ([source](https://github.com/urish/beat-machine))
  Full-featured instrument mixer (incl. horns/vocals). Use for: ear training beyond our local tool (`tools/beat-machine/`).
- [Videos: Addicted2Salsa — intermediate partnerwork](https://www.addicted2salsa.com/videos/tags/intermediate/)
  Free On1 partnerwork tutorials incl. cross-body lead variations. Use for: rebuilding lead repertoire.
- [Article: "Reverse Cross Body Lead (Right Side Pass)" — The Dance Dojo](https://thedancedojo.com/blog/how-to-do-the-reverse-cross-body-lead-right-side-pass/)
  Clear technique breakdowns with common-mistake lists. Use for: CBL family technique.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- Local salsa classes & socials (user already plans to attend)
  Use for: real-world testing of every lesson; ask experienced dancers for feedback on lead clarity.
- [r/Salsa](https://reddit.com/r/Salsa)
  Active, practitioner-heavy subreddit. Use for: social-dancing etiquette, plateau advice, scene questions.
