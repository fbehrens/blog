Welcome to my blog. This is where I write about things I find interesting.

## Why a blog?

There's something satisfying about owning your own corner of the internet. Social platforms come and go, but a simple website with markdown files is remarkably durable.

This site is built with Astro, hosted on Cloudflare Pages, and written in plain markdown. The source is on GitHub.

## What to expect

Short pieces. Code, photography, travel, observations. One tag per article to keep things loosely organized.
