# Context

## Glossary

**Subscriber**
A person who submitted the newsletter form *and* clicked the confirmation link. Only Subscribers receive newsletter emails.

**Pending Subscriber**
A person who submitted the subscription form but has not yet confirmed their email address. Not eligible to receive newsletters.

**Confirmation Email**
A transactional email sent to a Pending Subscriber containing a one-time link. Clicking it promotes them to Subscriber status (double opt-in).

**Newsletter**
An email sent to all Subscribers announcing a newly published blog post. Contains the post title, excerpt, and a link — not the full post body.

**Unsubscribe**
The act of a Subscriber opting out. Triggered via a unique link included in every Newsletter. Results in permanent removal from the Subscriber list.
